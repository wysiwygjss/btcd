# Send this ONE link to Mom & Dad

## https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/wysiwygjss/btcd@cursor/house-blueprint-3d-viewer-102b/house-tour/index.html

### What they do:
1. Tap the link (works on phone, tablet, or computer)
2. Tap **"Tap to Start Tour"**
3. Tap **"Guided Tour"** — the house visits each room automatically
4. Or tap room names (Kitchen, Bedroom, etc.) to jump there
5. Use the **arrow buttons** at the bottom to walk around
6. **Drag the screen** with finger to look around

No install. No password. No keyboard needed.
